About

I’m Omer Nachmani. I work on hardware and embedded security through independent research while completing my degree in electrical engineering.

Research interests

My current project studies power side-channel leakage from software AES running on a RISC-V processor. I am interested in how software execution, processor architecture, and physical hardware behavior interact, and how those interactions affect security.

I am developing this work toward firmware reverse engineering, exploitation, embedded and IoT security, hardware attacks, penetration testing, and application security. The writeup index records these planned directions.
