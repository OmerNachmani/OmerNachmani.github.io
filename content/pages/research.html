Power side-channel analysis on RISC-V

A B.Sc. project examining leakage from software AES-128 on a PicoRV32 processor implemented on FPGA. I developed the RTL integration and established a baseline for hardware countermeasure experiments.

The baseline recovered all 16 key bytes in two sessions, with independent ciphertext correctness checks. The next stage is to compare hardware protections and their implementation costs.
