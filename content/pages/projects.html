An Arduino Due obstacle-avoiding robot built around a fully non-blocking control loop. Combines interrupt-driven HC-SR04 ultrasonic sensing with median-of-three filtering, timed servo sweeps, software PWM motor control on an L293D driver, and a 2 kHz multiplexed seven-segment display.

A real-time environmental scanner built with an Arduino Uno, HC-SR04 ultrasonic sensor, SG90 servo motor, and PCD8544 LCD. Sweeps at 1-degree intervals to detect nearby objects and plots distance and angle data live on the display.
