Ongoing research

Power side-channel analysis on RISC-V

Software AES-128 on a PicoRV32 processor, implemented on a CW305 Artix-7 FPGA and measured with ChipWhisperer Husky. The baseline recovered all 16 key bytes in two sessions; hardware countermeasure comparisons are planned.
